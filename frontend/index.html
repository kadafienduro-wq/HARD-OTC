How it works

  1. Seller creates an offer. The tokens for sale are locked in the smart contract (escrow) and a listing fee is paid in HARD. The offer is public on the ledger.
  2. Buyer accepts. In one atomic transaction the buyer pays, receives the tokens, and the seller receives the payment directly to their account. If anything fails, nothing moves.
  3. Fees. Each side pays a trading fee (0.75% before discounts): the seller from the price, the buyer from the tokens received. Locked HARD lowers your side's fee.
  4. Cancel any time. The seller can cancel an open or expired offer and gets the tokens back. The listing fee is not refunded.
  5. Sell offers and buy requests. A sell offer locks the token being sold; a buy request locks the payment (e.g. XRD) and waits for someone who has the token. Both settle the same way.
  6. Partial fills. If the creator allows it, you can take only part of an offer. The rest stays on the market.
  7. Verified tokens (✓). Anyone can create a token with any name. ✓ means HARD ADV checked the address. Be careful with unverified tokens.
  8. Featuring (★). After posting, sellers can pay HARD per 24 hours to pin their offer at the top of the market. Fees go to the treasury.
  9. Reputation. Each HARD OTC Badge counts completed sales and purchases, shown next to every offer.
  10. We buy staked XRD. Sell your LSU to HARD ADV and get paid mostly in XRD plus a part in HARD, at a discount to its current value (shown on the “We buy staked XRD” tab). Requests are reviewed manually; you can cancel until then.
  11. Reserved offers. Holders with at least 1,000 HARD locked can pay an extra reservation fee in HARD to reserve an offer for a specific HARD OTC Badge number. Everyone can see it; only that badge holder can buy it.

Terms of every offer are public on the Radix ledger. This is an experimental testnet application.
